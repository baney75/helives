# Reader references (structure only, no pixels or marks borrowed)

- Standard Ebooks (standardebooks.org): one calm column, generous measure, colophon with sources and licenses; downloads offered in several formats per book.
- ESV.org reader: settings panel for size, font, verse numbers and headings on/off; chapter navigation at both ends.
- BibleProject book pages: each book leads with its overview video, then reading.
- CCEL (ccel.org): series, volume and author organization for the fathers; whole-volume downloads.
- Kindle / Readwise Reader "Aa" panel: theme, font, size, line spacing and margins grouped in one sheet near the thumb.
