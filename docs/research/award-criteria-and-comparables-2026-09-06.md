# He Lives: evaluation references and comparisons

Checked September 7, 2026. These references guide internal product review. They are not award submissions, predictions, or proof of recognition.

## Official criteria

### Awwwards

- [Mobile Excellence Guidelines](https://www.awwwards.com/mobile-excellence-guidelines.pdf): usability, performance, and mobile best practices. The practical checks for He Lives are readable text, clear navigation, restrained payloads, responsive behavior, and no essential interaction hidden behind motion.
- [Awwwards scored-site example](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/self-aware): the published scorecard separates Design, Usability, Creativity, and Content. Its developer review also names semantics/SEO, animation, accessibility, web-performance optimization, responsive design, and markup/metadata.

The scored page is evidence of Awwwards' published dimensions, not a promise that every entry receives the same weighting or a forecast for He Lives.

### The Webby Awards

- [2025/2026 Judging Criteria](https://www.webbyawards.com/judging-criteria/): Websites and Mobile Sites are considered across Content, Structure and Navigation, Visual Design, Functionality, Interactivity, Innovation, and Overall Experience. The criteria explicitly treat sound and animation as content, and they call for live links, quick loading, cross-platform behavior, inclusion, and support for varied bandwidth and access needs.
- [Judging Process](https://www.webbyawards.com/judging-process/): entries receive independent first-round review by multiple Associate Academy members before category-specific executive review. This supports He Lives' separate critic gate; it does not make the internal critic an award judge.

### CSS Design Awards

- [Official About page](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/about): the current public description centers UI, UX, and innovative development, with both jury and public award systems.
- [Published criteria explanation](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/blog/the-new-2014-css-design-awards-site/6/): the older official explanation groups review under Creativity + Design, Code + Functionality, and Usability + Content. Because this page describes the 2014 system, it is historical context rather than a claim about a current scoring formula.

## Comparable Scripture experiences

### VerseSee

[VerseSee](https://versesee.com/) builds passage-specific visual exhibits around Scripture, history, language, and geography. Useful comparison: let the text remain the anchor while each interaction earns its place by revealing structure or context.

### Behold

[Behold](https://www.comebehold.app/) combines reading, cross-references, timelines, journeys, typography controls, and offline use. Useful comparison: depth should stay navigable and user-controlled. He Lives should preserve its quieter, authored meditation rather than imitate a general study workspace.

### BibleVibe

[BibleVibe](https://www.biblevibe.ca/) combines reading, listening, visual stories, maps, and reflection. Useful comparison: multimodal Scripture work needs synchronized controls and a readable equivalent. Its AI-study model is not a requirement or a product direction for He Lives.

## Decisions applied

- Preserve the night, gold, and parchment system. Distinctiveness comes from the hourly lamp and spatial Genesis journey, not extra chrome.
- Make scene entry and return paths legible. Stable scene URLs, honest route status, and full-context KJV links matter more than decorative novelty.
- Keep audio explicit, captioned, and recoverable. Sound should support the text and never autoplay without a working user gate.
- Treat mobile readability, keyboard access, reduced motion, loading, missing assets, and WebGL fallback as product quality, not cleanup.
- Measure actual payload and runtime behavior. Do not call a local Lighthouse run field Core Web Vitals or claim broad accessibility compliance from automation.
