Frame

The site already confesses Nicene faith, quotes the KJV, and says it is not a church. Home already bears John 14:19. The name is a resurrection claim: He lives.

Westminster’s first question is settled doctrine: man’s chief end is to glorify God and enjoy him forever. Matthew 5:16 (KJV): “Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.” A website is a work. If the work points at itself — 3D flex, a music service, a login — it is not doing that job.

Deleted: study / background-music app, Spotify/YouTube stream research, $5/month catalog, Clerk-as-the-product, “Genesis bs” replacement-by-player. File removed: docs/plans/study-music-pwa.html.

Recommended

A — Witness: He lives. Go to church.

The whole public site is one proclamation. Resurrection, the verse you already chose, a short gospel, the Nicene paragraph you already wrote, a clear “find a church / go on Sunday.” No player. No 3D on the home path. Genesis can stay in the repo, unlinked, or sit as a quiet “meditation” link in the footer — not the product.

He Lives

because I live, ye shall live also.

Read why · Go to church

Why this is strong. It matches the name. It uses copy you already believe (FaithPage.tsx). It cannot be confused with YouVersion, Spotify, or a Bible video game. Craft still matters — type, space, the mark — but the visitor leaves knowing Christ lives and that they should sit under the Word in a real congregation.

Cost. Weeks, not a year of books. Clerk and PWA are optional later, not the point.

Risk. Looks “too small.” That is the point. A thin page that tells the truth beats a thick one that entertains.

B — Word: one passage, then church

A daily (or on-open) KJV passage with a short exhortation you write, never mixed into the verse. One prayer line. The same church invitation. No music. No infinite feed. Not a Bible app — YouVersion already exists. He Lives would be a lamp, not a library.
